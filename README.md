### 🔎 SQL Functions & Query Techniques

Throughout the project, I used a variety of SQL functions and clauses to solve business problems and generate reports:

#### WHERE Clause
Used **WHERE** to filter records based on specific conditions, such as:
- Finding products above a certain price
- Filtering customers by country
- Retrieving orders from specific years. 【1-b3e44d】

#### GROUP BY
Used **GROUP BY** to aggregate and summarise data:
- Counting products within categories
- Analysing grouped business data
- Producing category-level reporting. 【1-b3e44d】

#### ORDER BY
Used **ORDER BY** to sort results:
- Alphabetically by customer or product name
- By highest and lowest values
- By most recent order dates. 【1-b3e44d】

#### JOIN Operations
Developed an understanding of combining data across multiple related tables.

- 🔗 **INNER JOIN** – Retrieved matching records between tables
- 📋 **LEFT JOIN** – Returned all records from the left table and matching records from the right table
- 📑 **RIGHT JOIN** – Returned all records from the right table and matching records from the left table
- 🔄 **JOINs** used to combine:
  - Products and Suppliers
  - Products and Categories
  - Orders and Customers
  - Orders and Employees. 【1-b3e44d】
